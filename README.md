# Inception

## Introduction

*Inception* is a science fiction thriller about thieves who steal secrets by entering people's dreams. Directed by Christopher Nolan, the film is known for its layered dream worlds, striking visuals, and mind-bending plot. It became one of the most talked-about movies of its decade and is still widely discussed today.

- **Genre:** Science fiction, action, thriller
- **Release Year:** 2010

---

## Synopsis

Dom Cobb is a skilled thief who steals valuable secrets by entering people's dreams. Because of a past crime, he is unable to return home to his children. A powerful businessman named Saito offers him a chance to clear his name if he can perform the nearly impossible task of inception, which means planting an idea in someone's mind. Cobb assembles a team of specialists to enter the dream of Robert Fischer, the heir to a rival energy company. The team must travel through multiple dream levels, where time moves slower with each layer. Along the way, Cobb is haunted by memories of his late wife, Mal, who threatens to sabotage the mission. In the end, Cobb must face his guilt in order to complete the job and return to his family.

### Dream Levels

1. **Level One:** A rainy city, where the team kidnaps Fischer.
2. **Level Two:** A hotel, where the team continues the plan.
3. **Level Three:** A snowy mountain fortress, where the final mission takes place.
4. **Limbo:** A deep dream state where Cobb confronts his past.

## Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose *Inception* because of its original story and how it keeps me thinking long after the movie ends.

### Things I Liked

1. The **creative concept** of dreams within dreams.
2. The **visual effects**, especially the rotating hallway fight scene.
3. The **soundtrack** by Hans Zimmer, which makes every scene more intense.

### Rating

**9 / 10**

### Recommendation

> I recommend this movie to anyone who enjoys *thought-provoking* stories and action. It is worth watching more than once to catch every detail.
